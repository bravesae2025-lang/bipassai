FAQ

Common Questions

Does it bypass Turnitin and GPTZero?
Bipass AI rewrites your text to read like a real student — natural, varied, human — so it doesn't read like obvious AI. No tool can guarantee what an AI detector will say, but text that genuinely reads human is far less likely to stand out. More: Will your teacher know it's AI?
What are credits?
Level Matching uses 4 credits per word. Generated text is billed separately by output character. Analyzing your style is always free and doesn't cost credits.
What permissions does the Auto Typer need?
activeTab and scripting let it type into only the tab you choose after you press Start. storage keeps your sign-in and preferences in Chrome, and identity enables optional Google sign-in. Its website access is limited to Bipass AI. See the full explanation in the Privacy Policy or view the official Chrome Web Store listing.
Why does it need multiple writing samples?
More samples = better profile. One sample might just reflect the topic's style. Multiple samples let Bipass spot habits that are uniquely yours across different contexts.
Can I use it for any language?
Bipass AI works best with English. It can process other languages, but Level Matching is optimized for English text.
What's the difference between Level Matching and Generate?
Level Matching rewrites text you already have. Generate writes brand new text from a topic or prompt. Both use the same level settings and My Style profile.
Is my writing saved anywhere?
Your history and style profile are saved to your account. Bipass AI does not train its own model on your saved writing; text you ask us to process is sent to the model providers listed in our Privacy Policy.
