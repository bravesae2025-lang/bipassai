Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Who we are

Bipass AI is a writing tool that helps you match your text to a writing level and generate drafts. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the Bipass AI website and the Bipass AI Auto Typer browser extension.

What we collect

  • Account information — your chosen username, or your email address and display name when you sign in with Google.
  • Text results — the level-matched or generated text your account produces, stored so you can access your history and use the Auto Typer extension.
  • Authentication tokens — access and refresh tokens stored locally in your browser (via chrome.storage.local for the extension and Supabase browser storage for the website) to keep you signed in.
  • Usage tier — whether your account is on the free or paid plan, stored in your account metadata.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not read the content of pages you visit.
  • The Auto Typer extension only injects into the active tab when you explicitly press Start — it does not run in the background on other pages.

How we use your data

  • To authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
  • To send text you ask us to process to the selected writing-model provider and return the result.
  • To store and display your text results in History and the extension.
  • To track your credit balance and plan status.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share only the data needed with the service providers listed below to operate the product.

Browser storage and cookies

The website uses browser storage that is needed to provide features you request:

  • Supabase authentication storage keeps your access and refresh tokens so you remain signed in.
  • Local storage remembers settings such as writing level, model, saved writing styles, onboarding progress, privacy choices, and dismissed notices.
  • Session storage temporarily carries your draft, selected options, and generated result between app screens in the same browser tab.

Google Analytics is optional and does not load unless you accept it. If accepted, Google Analytics (GA4) sets first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* and receives usage data including pages visited, approximate location, device, and browser type. We disable Google advertising signals and set Analytics cookies to expire after no more than 12 months without extending that period on each visit.

You can accept or reject Analytics from the privacy panel without affecting access to Bipass AI. You can change your choice later through Cookie settings in the website footer or the Privacy section in Settings. Rejecting after previously accepting stops future Analytics collection on Bipass AI and removes the Analytics cookies available to our site.

Payment, sign-in, hosting, and security providers may use strictly necessary storage on their own pages or domains under their policies. You can remove all website storage through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out, reset preferences, or remove drafts that have not been saved to your account.

Auto Typer extension permissions

The Auto Typer requests four Chrome permissions. Each is used for a specific feature:

  • activeTab — identifies only the tab you choose when you start the Auto Typer.
  • scripting — loads the typing helper into that active tab after you press Start.
  • storage — stores your sign-in session and extension preferences locally in Chrome.
  • identity — opens Chrome's secure authentication flow when you choose Google sign-in.

The extension's host access is limited to bipassai.com and www.bipassai.com for account and result access. It does not receive permission to read every website continuously. You can inspect the current permission list on the official Chrome Web Store listing.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account data and results are stored on Supabase servers. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — used to process payments for passes and credit packs. We do not store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — receives website usage data only after you accept optional Analytics. Advertising signals are disabled. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google OAuth — used as an optional sign-in method. We only receive your email and display name.
  • Google Gemini — processes text for writing-level matching, generation, and style analysis.
  • Anthropic Claude — may process generated text for an additional rewrite pass when that feature is enabled.

Data retention

Your text results are kept until you delete them from History. Auth tokens are cleared when you sign out. If you delete your account, all associated data is removed.

Your rights

You can delete results from History and delete your account from Settings. To request a copy of your data, contact us at the address below.
