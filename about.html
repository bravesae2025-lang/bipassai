About Bipass AI

Level matching
that learns your style.

Choose a writing level, build a profile from past work, then Auto Type the reviewed result into Google Docs.

Bipass AI analyzes your vocabulary, grammar, punctuation, and sentence patterns, then rewrites AI drafts to match your real writing level.

You can use a calibrated preset — Beginner, Student, Academic, or Custom — or create a Writing Profile from 1–5 samples of your own work. The profile captures the patterns already present in your writing and uses them as guidance for later rewrites.

01
Match a chosen level

One focused rewriting workflow

Paste a draft, choose the level that fits the work, and select Match My Level. Bipass AI adjusts vocabulary, grammar, punctuation, spelling, tense, capitalization, and sentence rhythm while preserving the original meaning.

02
Learn from past writing

Writing Profiles match patterns that are already yours

Add samples you wrote previously. Bipass AI analyzes vocabulary difficulty, tone, grammar habits, punctuation, spelling, capitalization, tense, and sentence construction. You can review and adjust those traits before applying the profile.

Sample analysis is free. Your samples should be genuinely yours and representative of the level you want to match.

03
Review, then type

Auto Typer for Google Docs

Review the rewrite and its highlighted changes first. Then upload the finished text to the Chrome extension and let Auto Typer enter it character by character. You can also send your own finished text directly without rewriting it.

Clear expectations

Level matching is not a detector guarantee

Closer level and style matching may make text less likely to raise obvious AI suspicion, but detector scores vary and no result is guaranteed. Detectors can produce false positives, and a score is not proof of authorship. Review the result, verify facts and citations, add your own reasoning, and follow the rules that apply to your work.

Frequently asked

What to know before you start

How much does Level Matching cost?
Exactly 4 credits per input word. Creating or refreshing a Writing Profile is free.
Can it match my own writing?
Yes. Create a Writing Profile from 1–5 past samples, check the analyzed traits, and select that profile as your level.
Does Auto Typer replace review?
No. Auto Typer is a convenience tool for text you have already reviewed. It does not recreate an authentic research or drafting process.
